# IT Troubleshooting Guide

## Slow computer
Restart the device and close applications that are not required. If the problem continues, contact IT support.

## Application not responding
Where appropriate, close and reopen the affected application. If the issue continues, record the error message and contact IT support.

## Password forgotten
Use the approved password reset process or contact IT support. Never send your password to another person.

## Suspected phishing
Do not disclose credentials or follow suspicious instructions. Report suspected phishing emails to IT immediately.

## Lost company device
Report a lost or stolen company device as soon as possible.

## High-priority incidents
An incident that prevents an employee from working should be marked as high priority when submitting the IT support request.
