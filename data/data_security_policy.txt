Hatfield Technologies Ltd
Data Security Policy

Employees are responsible for protecting company information and systems.

Passwords must not be shared with other people.

Employees should use strong and unique passwords for company systems.

Multi-factor authentication must be used where it is available.

Confidential company information should only be accessed by authorised employees.

Employees must lock their computer when leaving it unattended.

Sensitive company information must not be copied to personal USB drives or personal cloud storage accounts.

Suspicious emails, phishing attempts, malware, and suspected unauthorised access must be reported to IT immediately.

Lost or stolen company devices should be reported as soon as possible.

Employees should avoid discussing confidential business information in public places.
