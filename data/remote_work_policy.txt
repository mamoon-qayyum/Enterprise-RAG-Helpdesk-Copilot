Hatfield Technologies Ltd
Remote Working Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week.

Remote working days must be agreed in advance with the employee's line manager.

Employees must remain available during the company's core working hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Employees working remotely are expected to maintain normal levels of productivity and communication.

Company-issued devices should be used when accessing confidential or sensitive company information.

Employees must not use public computers to access internal company systems.

Employees should use secure password-protected Wi-Fi when working remotely.

The company may temporarily suspend remote working arrangements where there are business, performance, security, or operational concerns.
