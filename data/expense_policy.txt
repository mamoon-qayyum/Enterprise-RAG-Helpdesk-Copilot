Hatfield Technologies Ltd
Business Expense Policy

Employees may claim reasonable expenses that are incurred wholly for approved business purposes.

Employees should obtain manager approval before making significant business purchases.

Expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred.

Receipts or other appropriate evidence must be provided for expense claims wherever possible.

Reasonable travel, accommodation, and meal costs may be reimbursed when employees are travelling for approved company business.

Personal expenses will not be reimbursed.

Alcohol and entertainment expenses require prior management approval.

The finance team may reject incomplete, unsupported, excessive, or non-business-related expense claims.
