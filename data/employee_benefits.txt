Hatfield Technologies Ltd
Employee Benefits

Eligible employees have access to a range of company benefits.

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave in addition to public holidays.

The company operates a workplace pension scheme for eligible employees.

Employees may access an employee assistance programme that provides confidential wellbeing support.

The company offers professional development support for approved training courses and certifications.

Employees may request flexible or remote working arrangements in accordance with company policy.

A cycle-to-work scheme is available to eligible employees.

Specific benefit eligibility may depend on employment status, length of service, and the terms of the employee's contract.
