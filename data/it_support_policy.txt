Hatfield Technologies Ltd
IT Support Policy

Employees experiencing IT problems should first restart the affected device or application where appropriate.

If the issue continues, employees should contact the internal IT support team.

IT support requests should include the employee's name, device, a description of the problem, and any relevant error message.

Employees who forget their password should use the approved password reset process or contact IT support.

Employees must never share their password with another employee, manager, or IT support representative.

Urgent incidents that prevent an employee from working should be marked as high priority.

Suspected security incidents, phishing emails, lost devices, or unauthorised access must be reported to IT immediately.
