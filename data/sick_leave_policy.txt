Hatfield Technologies Ltd
Sick Leave Policy

Employees who are unable to work due to illness must notify their line manager as soon as possible and preferably before 9:00 AM on the first day of absence.

Employees may self-certify sickness for absences of up to 7 calendar days.

For sickness lasting more than 7 calendar days, employees must provide a medical certificate or fit note.

Employees should keep their manager updated if the expected return-to-work date changes.

Where appropriate, the company may arrange a return-to-work meeting after a period of sickness absence.

Repeated or long-term sickness absence may be reviewed by HR in accordance with company procedures.

Employees should not attend the workplace if doing so may create a health risk for colleagues.
