Hatfield Technologies Ltd
Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, excluding public holidays.

Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis.

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 7 calendar days before the requested leave date.

All leave requests must be approved by the employee's line manager.

Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year.

Any unused leave above the five-day carry-forward limit will expire at the end of the calendar year unless special approval is granted by HR.

Employees who leave the organisation will receive payment for any accrued but unused annual leave where applicable.

During particularly busy business periods, managers may refuse or request changes to leave dates if adequate team coverage cannot be maintained.
