Hatfield Technologies Ltd
Working Hours Policy

The standard working week for full-time employees is 37.5 hours.

Normal office hours are Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM.

Employees are entitled to a one-hour unpaid lunch break.

The company's core working hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Flexible start and finish times may be permitted with manager approval.

Employees must accurately record their working hours where required.

Overtime should normally be approved by a manager before the additional hours are worked.

Employees should take appropriate rest breaks and should not routinely work excessive hours.
